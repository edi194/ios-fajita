# Bergamota identity (approved by the user, 2026-10-24)

Face: Isaac Newton, after Godfrey Kneller's 1689 portrait (public domain; Wikimedia Commons
"File:GodfreyKneller-IsaacNewton-1689.jpg"), cut out and printed as a black-and-white halftone
(round dots, ~6.5 px cell at 1280 px wide, ink #161616 on paper #f4f3ec). No red, no tint on the figure.
Like IDA uses Ada Lovelace: the portrait IS the logo.

Palette (colourway B):
- ground: lime #d7e98a with a halftone dot pattern #c6dc72 (1.6 px dots, 7 px grid)
- giant numerals / highlights: white #ffffff (e.g. the iOS version "2" "6" tucked behind the figure)
- ink: #161616, paper #f4f3ec
- action / accent: leaf green #1a5933 (primary buttons)
Type: Archivo (variable, heavy 850-900, slightly expanded wdth ~112 for display), JetBrains Mono for data/code.

Files here:

- newton.psd           Photopea document (layer "Newton halftone"; two colour layers present but not used)
- newton-kneller-1689-source.jpg  source painting
- v2.html + newton-final.webp     the approved web preview (landing + flasher)
Logo: crop the head (roughly x 300-1000, y 200-1000 of the master) on the lime halftone ground, rounded square.
